Funding workflow

How an Asset Becomes Trust-Controlled

1
Identify

Describe the asset, current owner, and intended Trust treatment.

2
Review

Check legal, tax, lender, insurance, contract, and beneficiary effects.

3
Execute

Complete the proper deed, assignment, title, designation, or institution form.

4
Verify & Record

Confirm ownership, then update the asset schedule, binder, and digital index.

□Confirm the asset is eligible and appropriate for Trust ownership.
□Use the exact Trust and Trustee name shown in executed documents.
□Retain signed originals or certified copies in the secure vault.
□Do not mark “funded” until ownership or beneficiary status is verified.
Change control

Amendments and Legal Changes

1
Request

State the proposed change, reason, affected people/assets, and target date.

2
Impact Review

Review Trust, community-property, tax, business, insurance, and succession effects.

3
Execute

Use the formal signing, consent, acknowledgment, and delivery method required.

4
Synchronize

Update certifications, schedules, LLC records, website, binder, and archive.

Texts, verbal statements, casual notes, and website edits do not change the Trust.
